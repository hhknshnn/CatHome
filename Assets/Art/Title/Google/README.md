# Google sign-in mark

`GoogleSignIn_G_Square_Light.png` is the official, pre-approved Android/Web
light square asset from Google's **Sign in with Google** branding bundle:

https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip

Do not recolour, crop, stretch or redraw the Google `G`. Cat Home may style the
surrounding button and halo, but the asset itself must stay on its supplied white
background and retain a square aspect ratio.
